# Ziggy — arte e animação

Arquivo usado pelo site: `assets/ziggy-sprites-v1.png` (1024 × 1536, PNG RGBA).

Produzido pela ferramenta integrada **image_gen**, sem CLI ou chave de API do projeto. A prancha ilustrada aprovada na conversa foi usada como referência. As fotos originais, pessoas e ambiente doméstico não foram copiadas para o repositório.

O atlas contém quatro quadros por linha e seis linhas: atento/piscando, acompanhando a digitação, pensando, respondendo, surpreso e descansando. O CSS troca os quadros reais; não se limita a mover uma imagem estática. Pequenas diferenças de desenho entre os quadros são próprias da ilustração gerada.

`ziggy.js` controla os estados da interface; `ziggy.css` mostra o atlas. A preparação visual de resposta dura cerca de 480ms. A soneca começa após 45 segundos no estado de espera. Há pausa manual e respeito a `prefers-reduced-motion`. A expressão de surpresa indica resposta local não reconhecida ou falha; não é análise emocional por IA.

## Prompt de geração

Create a production animation SPRITE ATLAS of the EXACT Ziggy character shown in the provided concept sheet. Preserve his recognizable scruffy golden caramel terrier face, creamy moustache, dark soft eyes, black nose, floppy ears, navy blue bandana with tiny gold botanical edge. Same polished softly shaded 2D illustration. This is NOT a presentation board: absolutely NO text, titles, labels, lines, borders, checkerboard, ground, shadows or backgrounds. GENUINE TRANSPARENT RGBA background. EXACTLY 4 equal columns and 6 equal rows = 24 separate square tiles in a perfectly regular rectangular grid, overall canvas aspect ratio 2:3 (ideally 1024x1536). Each tile contains ONE consistent head-and-shoulders bust of Ziggy. Every dog fully inside its own square tile with at least 12 percent transparent padding on all sides. All tiles identical character scale and same neck/chest baseline, head centered horizontally. No overlap across cells. Four columns are four sequential ANIMATION FRAMES of the SAME state, left to right; six rows are six states, top to bottom. ROW1 attentive neutral breathing and blinking: eyes open, half closed, closed blink, open. ROW2 listening to typed question with slight head tilt and ear motion: small left tilt, neutral, small right tilt, neutral. ROW3 thinking, looking upward: upper-left gaze, upper-center gaze, upper-right gaze, center gaze. ROW4 speaking happily: mouth gently closed, mouth slightly open, mouth open showing small pink tongue, mouth slightly open, warm engaged eyes. ROW5 surprised/confused yet friendly: eyebrows raised eyes wide, mild head tilt, gently open mouth, return toward neutral. ROW6 resting/sleeping: eyelids half closed, eyes closed head slightly lowered, eyes closed breathing out, half closed. Subtle smooth pose changes suitable for looping, stable scale and registration, no extra body motion or props, no duplication errors. Use the DOG ONLY from the input sheet as reference; discard all layout, background and text. Pixel-clean separate cutout sprites, high quality fur silhouettes.

Result is the same 24-frame animation atlas with true transparent empty space in every tile for display on a deep navy website.
